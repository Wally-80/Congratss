# Congratss Store Assets and Copy Pack

Last updated: 2026-03-06

## Brand direction

Congratss should present as:

- warm, celebratory, organized
- useful for remembering people, not just dates
- fast to share, not bloated

Visual direction:

- dark premium base with bright celebration accents
- people-first moments, not generic corporate calendar screens
- use the real app UI in every screenshot

## Required assets

### Apple

- app icon embedded in Xcode asset catalog
- 1 to 10 screenshots per device family
- optional app previews, up to 3 per localization

Recommended for Congratss:

- 5 iPhone screenshots
- 5 iPad screenshots only if the native app is shipped as universal
- 1 app preview video later, not required for first submission

### Google Play

- app icon: 512x512 PNG
- feature graphic: 1024x500 JPG or PNG
- minimum 2 screenshots to publish
- recommended 4+ high-resolution phone screenshots
- optional YouTube preview video

Recommended for Congratss:

- 6 phone screenshots
- 2 tablet screenshots if tablet layout is supported well
- 1 feature graphic
- no preview video in v1 unless you have polished native flows
